# Seedance 2.0 — "The More You Suppress" (9:16 Short)

Allegorical protest sequence for Instagram/YouTube Shorts.
**Status: prompt ready, not generated. 67.5 credits when approved.**

---

## Direction notes

Framed as **suppression and resilience**, not roaches attacking police. The
riot line advances, the cockroach-students absorb the charge, go down, and
rise with scarlet fissures spreading through their cracked shells — then the
street fills from every drain and side lane.

Two reasons for that framing over a straight brawl: it matches the message
("the more you suppress, the harder it erupts"), and a clip of protesters
assaulting police would read as advocating violence against law enforcement,
which is both a legal problem and off-message for the brand.

**Guardrail compliance:** the riot line is deliberately faceless and
featureless — matte-black gear, blank rectangular shields, no insignia, no
badge numbers, no unit markings, no flag, no emblem, no text on any surface.
Nothing in frame identifies a real force or a real person.

**Palette:** charcoal and off-white, with Election Red `#C8331C` as the only
saturated colour — carried on the banner, then on the fissures in the shells.

## EN prompt

```
Style & Mood: Arcane-inspired painterly 3D animation — visible brushstroke texture on every surface, hand-painted grain, hard chiaroscuro. Charcoal blacks and off-white haze dominate; a single scarlet accent recurs on cloth and light. Volumetric dust and tear-gas haze, anamorphic flare, shallow depth. Vertical 9:16 framing. Grim, defiant, escalating. Dynamic Description: Static low-angle wide, camera locked at street level — a rank of faceless armoured figures in featureless matte-black riot gear stands shoulder to shoulder behind blank rectangular shields, no markings or emblems anywhere on them, transparent visors reflecting nothing. Facing them from the right, a loose cluster of anthropomorphic cockroach figures in student clothing holds the line, one raising a scarlet cloth banner overhead. Hard cut to handheld medium close-up, camera shuddering — the shield wall surges forward left-to-right, shields slamming into the cockroach figures, chitinous shells buckling, several driven to their knees, the scarlet banner dropping below frame. Cut to a locked-off extreme close-up insert: the fallen banner-bearer's segmented hand pressed flat into wet asphalt, fingers slowly closing around the scarlet fabric. Cut to stabilized tracking, rising vertically — the kneeling figure stands, then the ones beside it, shells cracked with luminous scarlet fissures spreading along the fractures like heated metal. Hard cut to crane ascent, extreme wide — the armoured rank now small at frame bottom, while cockroach figures pour up out of storm drains, doorways and side lanes into the street, the risen banner burning scarlet against the haze. Static Description: Narrow urban street at night, shuttered storefronts, a high painted compound wall, sodium streetlamps in thick haze. Wet asphalt, scattered rubble, overturned barricades. Torn paper drifting. Blank unmarked riot gear, plain rectangular shields, no insignia, no flags, no emblems, no text on any surface.
```
