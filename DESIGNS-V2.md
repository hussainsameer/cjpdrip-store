## Non-negotiable guardrail

Locked per the decision on this drop, and consistent with `README.md`
("designs deliberately avoid naming any individual — they target ideas and the
system") and the four products already legally pulled.

Every design in this drop **must**:

- Target **systems, processes and institutions** — never a named or recognisable individual
- Contain **no political party symbol** (no lotus, hand, broom, cycle, or any
  Election Commission allotted symbol)
- Contain **no national flag, tricolour, Ashoka Chakra, or State Emblem**
  (the lions are separately protected under the State Emblem of India Act 2005 —
  this is what killed Tricolor Roach and the Manifesto Tee)
- Contain **no real police/force insignia, unit name, or badge number**
- Use the cockroach as the **everyman protagonist**, not as a caricature of a person

If a concept can only be understood as being about one specific human being,
it does not ship in this drop.

## Credit slot

Every design carries a small credit line — **back yoke, below the neck seam**,
12mm cap height, single colour.

Default for this drop:

```
@cockroach_janta_party.merch
```

The slot is designed so a **collaborating artist's handle** drops in beside it
once a proper licence or paid collab exists via the `/artists` page:

```
@cockroach_janta_party.merch  ×  @artist_handle
```

**Do not print a third party's handle on a design they did not make or licence.**
It implies authorship and endorsement they never gave.

# A · Protest Ink + Red Spot

High-contrast brush and ink. Charcoal on Off-White. Election Red used on exactly
one element per design. Screen-print friendly — two-colour separation.

### A1 · Baat Karne Se Dar Lagta Hai?
- **slug:** `baat-karne-se-dar`
- **tagline:** CONVERSATION IS THE CHEAPEST DEMAND
- **art:** A single small roach-person, front-on, holding a placard above its head.
  Bearing down from the top-right, a dense wall of raked diagonal baton strokes —
  drawn as pure black ink slashes, no hands, no uniforms, no faces. The placard is
  the only red object in the frame. Massive scale mismatch: the figure occupies the
  bottom-left eighth of the composition.
- **devanagari on placard:** बात करने से डर लगता है?
- **why it's safe:** no insignia, no identifiable person; the aggressor is rendered
  as abstract geometry, not a force.

### A2 · Antenna Up
- **slug:** `antenna-up`
- **tagline:** SIGNAL RECEIVED
- **art:** A raised-fist silhouette where the forearm resolves, on second read, into
  a cockroach antenna curving up out of a shell. Single-weight ink, no shading.
  Red only on a thin horizontal underline beneath the mark.

### A3 · Survived the Charge
- **slug:** `survived-the-charge`
- **tagline:** SHELL CRACKED. STILL WALKING.
- **art:** A cockroach carapace in three-quarter view, split by hairline fractures.
  The cracks are Election Red, drawn like kintsugi seams. The insect is upright and
  mid-stride, not collapsed. Ink hatching for the shell's curvature.

### A4 · Suppress / Erupt
- **slug:** `suppress-erupt`
- **tagline:** THE MORE YOU PRESS, THE HARDER IT BLOWS
- **art:** A heavy anonymous gauntleted hand — no cuff insignia, no skin tone,
  pure black shape — pressing down on a pressure cooker lid. Roaches and steam
  blast out from every side of the seal. Red only in the flame beneath.
- **note:** the reference cartoon put a recognisable face here. This one deliberately
  does not. The hand is a shape, not a person.

### A5 · Night Shift
- **slug:** `night-shift`
- **tagline:** WE LIVE HERE NOW
- **art:** Wide, low horizontal composition. Four or five roach-people asleep on
  pavement flagstones beneath a high compound wall, bags under heads. One is awake,
  sitting up, watching. Sparse ink, lots of Off-White. Red only on the awake one's
  eye-glint.

Painterly, muted, strange. The premium tier — best print quality, hero pieces.
Follows the cockroach-lotus register: unsettling rather than funny.

## Generation plan

**Model:** `nano_banana_2` @ 1.5 cr/image, 1k, 4:5 portrait for artwork
(chest-print aspect), 4:5 for mockups.
Returns JPEG — re-encode to PNG before committing (known issue).

**Two passes:**

1. **Flat artwork** ×20 → `public/designs/<slug>.png`
   Transparent or flat-colour ground, print-ready, no garment.
2. **Mockups** ×20 → `public/mockups/<slug>.png`
   The approved artwork placed on a garment, shot on a model.

**Mockup direction:** young Indian adults, 20s, street casual, shot in real Indian
urban settings — chai stall, metro platform, painted compound wall, night street
under sodium light. Natural available light, mild grain, documentary rather than
catalogue. Garment colours drawn from the locked palette. Never a white-cyclorama
studio look — that reads corporate and kills the brand voice.

**Style suffix — append verbatim to every artwork prompt:**

```
Flat vector-screenprint artwork for a t-shirt chest print. Limited palette:
charcoal #1A1714 and off-white #F5F1E8 with a single accent of election red
#C8331C used sparingly. Bold poster composition, generous negative space,
no gradients, no photorealism, no drop shadows. Subject centred with clear
margins on all sides. No background scenery. No text other than specified.
No national flag, no political party symbol, no government emblem, no police
insignia, no recognisable real person.
```
